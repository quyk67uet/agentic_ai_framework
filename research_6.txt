### 1. Key Concepts and Definitions

*   **Definition:** Six is a natural number following 5 and preceding 7. It is an integer and a whole number.
*   **Mathematical Properties:**
    *   It is a composite number, its proper divisors being 1, 2, and 3.
    *   It is the smallest perfect number: the sum of its proper divisors (1 + 2 + 3) equals 6. A perfect number is a positive integer that is equal to the sum of its proper divisors (excluding the number itself).
    *   6 is a harmonic divisor number, meaning the harmonic mean of its divisors is an integer. The divisors of 6 are 1, 2, 3, and 6. The harmonic mean is 4 / (1/1 + 1/2 + 1/3 + 1/6) = 4 / (12/12 + 6/12 + 4/12 + 2/12) = 4 / (24/12) = 4 / 2 = 2, which is an integer.
    *   It is the product of the first two natural numbers (1 x 2 x 3 = 6) and also the sum (1 + 2 + 3 = 6).
    *   6 is a highly composite number, meaning it has more divisors than any smaller positive integer.
    *   It is a factorial (3! = 3 x 2 x 1 = 6).  Factorials are used extensively in combinatorics and probability.
    *   In base 10, a number is divisible by 6 if it is divisible by both 2 and 3. This is a fundamental divisibility rule taught in elementary arithmetic.
    *   A six-sided polygon is a hexagon. Hexagons have unique properties, such as being able to tessellate a plane.
    *   A six-sided solid is a hexahedron (e.g., a cube). A cube is a regular hexahedron, one of the Platonic solids.
*   **Mersenne Primes and Perfect Numbers:** Euclid proved that if 2^p − 1 is prime (a Mersenne prime), then 2^(p−1) * (2^p − 1) is a perfect number. For p=2, 2^2-1 = 3, so 2^(2-1) * (2^2 - 1) = 2 * 3 = 6. The search for Mersenne primes is an ongoing area of research in number theory.
